IMPORTANT — extraction method differs from data/public_16/
  TADB3 relBE spans Cyanobacteria, Actinobacteria, Firmicutes, Vibrio,
  Helicobacter, Caulobacter etc. The 527 bp Enterobacteriaceae seed
  (anchors/relBE_seed.fa) will NOT blast to most of these. Loci are therefore
  located by LOCUS TAG from the table above, not by seed homology.
  This is a deliberate departure from the Stage 1-3 workflow.
